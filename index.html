Stressed, wired, or feeling out of balance — could Healy help?

That's exactly the moment Healy is made for. Healy uses individualized microcurrent frequencies (IMF programs) chosen in the app to support balance for mind and body and help you unwind. You clip it on and run a relaxation or balance program while you rest, read, or work — it's wellness support for daily life, not a cure or a replacement for medical care. What is Healy, in 30 seconds?

Healy is a small, certified wearable you clip to your clothes. It connects by Bluetooth to the Healy app on your phone, where you pick a program — for example relaxation, focus, energy, or your evening routine, depending on your edition. The device then runs that individualized frequency program while you carry on with your day.

Do I need a clinic, wires everywhere, or a whole new routine?

No. There's no clinic visit and no complicated setup. Charge Healy like a phone, clip it on, connect the app, and choose a program. Most people run their first program within minutes of unboxing, at home, at work, or while travelling.

How is Healy different from a fitness tracker or smartwatch?

A tracker mainly measures — steps, heart rate, sleep. Healy is different: instead of just tracking, it delivers individualized frequency programs through the app, designed to support bioenergetic balance, focus, and stress relief. Many people use both: a watch to track, Healy as their wearable wellness tool.
